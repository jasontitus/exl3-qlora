2. Train a pilot

Start with two updates to check the workflow. More updates are needed to assess learning; validation chooses the best saved adapter.

Stopping safely can take several minutes. Completed checkpoints support exact optimizer resume.

3. Compare and try

Uses held-out test data, or labels validation-only results as development. Loss improvement alone does not establish task quality.
