# Shared GPU lease for the serial M5 campaign

`shared_gpu_lease_v2.py` preserves the control-qualified original wrapper. It fixes a long-run PID-reuse gap: a newly born descendant is tracked when its ancestry is currently owned, even if its PID was previously used. An unrelated reused PID is ignored; a changed leader identity fails closed. A newly observed member of the original leader group after leader exit is uncertain and keeps the lease unless a live owned parent proves ancestry. The receipt retains observed birth generations.

37 CPU tests and five subtests pass, including five serial phases with 15 nested-session children and a reused child surviving after its launcher exits. These are process-table fixtures and temporary filesystem ownership tests, not an actual long training run. Independent review and a concrete prepared-plan review remain required. See `shared-gpu-lease-v2-implementation.json` for exact source/test bindings and request requirements.

The unchanged `run_m5_plan.py` synchronously emits, runs and verifies each of its five phases. New process sessions preserve parent ancestry. The SFT training process also waits for its workflow child. The outer lease covers the whole serial campaign and releases only after successful completion and no observed live descendants. Its required completion status is `completed_pending_quality_audit`, with `quality_certified: false`; this proves operational completion, not improved model quality.

The outer wrapper never signals processes. The inner reviewed runner can signal its own children on a phase failure. A failed, interrupted or uncertain campaign keeps the lease and recovery receipt, even when cleanup may already have completed; recovery requires explicit ownership/process inspection. Interrupting the wrapper alone does not stop its training child.

Before wrapping a prepared plan, finish the model transfer and full-byte verification. Pin the actual planned Python executable and resolved target, launcher, immutable plan, helper, runtime snapshot, completed payload receipt and its source manifest. Use the exact plan cwd, a fresh inner completion receipt and separate fresh wrapper outputs. Do not pin mutable project state, checkpoints, logs or cache files; the inner launcher checks the initial project hash and guards its own immutable inputs. No placeholder SHA is an execution authorization.

Invoke the wrapper with `MLX_ENABLE_TF32=0` inherited and no other `MLX_*` variables. The wrapper itself only sets `GPU_LOCK_DIR`; the inner launcher enforces the numerical environment. Request `[0]` as the only accepted exit and match the exact plan/hash, payload receipt/hash, launch format, numerical environment, `completed_pending_quality_audit` and `quality_certified: false` fields. The detailed request contract is recorded in the implementation receipt. No GPU lease should cover transfer, source editing, compilation or unrelated work.

Process sampling is conservative evidence for cooperative, reviewed, synchronously waited children. It is not proof against malicious daemonization, same-second PID reuse or a hostile operating system. All preceding control receipts remain bound to the unchanged original wrapper.
