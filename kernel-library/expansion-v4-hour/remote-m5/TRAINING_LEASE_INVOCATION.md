# Concrete training launch lifecycle

This is the deployment procedure for a separately reviewed, concrete training request. It adds no launcher implementation and does not authorize execution of placeholders. The full model copy, full-byte verification, prepared plan, exact wrapper request and their hashes must be complete and reviewed first. `shared_gpu_lease_v2.py` and the unchanged `run_m5_plan.py` remain responsible for ownership and campaign checks.

Before starting, verify the actual hashes of the reviewed Python executable and its resolved target, v2 wrapper, request, plan, runner, helper, runtime snapshot, completed payload receipt and its source manifest. Check the canonical host/cwd and all required fresh output paths. The environment inherited by the wrapper must contain exactly `MLX_ENABLE_TF32=0` among `MLX_*` variables. A read-only free-lock observation is useful but cannot replace the wrapper's atomic acquisition.

Use a short reviewed Python invocation on the M5 to open dedicated fresh stdout/stderr files exclusively and start the wrapper with `subprocess.Popen(start_new_session=True, stdin=subprocess.DEVNULL, stdout=stdout_file, stderr=stderr_file, cwd=canonical_cwd, env=reviewed_environment)`. Its argv is the actual planned Python, exact v2 wrapper path, `run`, `--request`, sealed request path, `--sha256`, actual request SHA256, `--receipt`, and the fresh wrapper receipt path. Do not wrap a shell command or add unrelated work. A new session and detached input/output prevent an ordinary SSH disconnect from interrupting the long campaign.

Persist a separate launch receipt with the full argv, cwd, environment relevant to numerical/ownership behavior, all checked input hashes, canonical log paths, creation time, returned wrapper PID and observed process-start identity. Confirm the observed identity while that child is live; if it already exited, record the exit and inspect its failure instead of reporting an active campaign. The process-start observation is local lifecycle evidence, not host attestation. Output logs have fixed exclusive paths; their final byte hashes are recorded after completion rather than pretending their future contents were pre-hashed.

An optional separate `/usr/bin/caffeinate -i -w <wrapper_pid>` may prevent idle sleep while that exact live wrapper runs on AC power. Record its PID/argv too. It is not a GPU workload or ownership mechanism, and must not keep running as an unrelated campaign after the wrapper exits. It should not be inserted into the model argv or the lease's declared completion criteria.

Poll receipts and exact recorded process identities read-only. Do not restart on SSH loss, a missing progress update or a failed phase. On success, the inner receipt must say `completed_pending_quality_audit` and `quality_certified: false`; then the outer wrapper can confirm clean descendants and release its own lease. On failure or uncertain interruption the wrapper retains its lease and recovery receipt. Inspect that specific campaign's children before any explicitly authorized recovery; never delete another owner's lock or kill unrelated jobs. A detached wrapper can continue after the controlling terminal disappears, so ending SSH is not a stop request.

No actual training invocation, GPU computation or model payload read was performed while writing this procedure. A concrete invocation must bind the actual fixed final checkpoint/step plan and must not be substituted with an arbitrary adapter selection.
